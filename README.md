# AmazingMart EU Sales Analysis

# Overview

This project analyzes transactional data from AmazingMart EU to uncover patterns in sales, customer behavior, product performance, and profitability. Using both Tableau visualizations and analytical reasoning, the goal is to deliver actionable business insights that can improve marketing, inventory, and regional strategy.

# Dataset

The analysis is based on three interconnected sheets:

ListOfOrders: Order-level information including customer, location, and shipping.

OrderBreakdown: Line-item details such as product, quantity, discount, sales, and profit.

SalesTargets: Monthly sales goals for each product category.

# Key Business Questions

Which product categories and sub-categories generate the most revenue and profit?

How do customer segments (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) differ in behavior?

Which regions or countries are underperforming or overachieving relative to sales targets?

How do discounts affect profit margins?

Are monthly sales trends in line with business goals?

# Visualizations Included (in Tableau Workbook)

# Monthly Order Trends by Year

Seasonal peaks identified in August and November

Year-over-year growth trend indicates increasing customer engagement

# Orders by Country and Region

High performance in France, Germany, UK, Italy, and Spain

Low-activity countries flagged for further market analysis

# Orders by Segment and Sub-Category

Consumer segment dominates, especially in Office Supplies

Targeted bundling and seasonal marketing opportunities identified

# Sales vs Target by Category

All categories met or exceeded targets

Technology and Office Supplies exceeded goals substantially

# Sales vs Profit by Customer

Profitability not always aligned with revenue

Discounting and fulfillment cost issues highlighted

# Key Insights

Growth Opportunities: Invest in high-performing countries and categories.

Profitability Risks: Address negative-profit customers through better discount policies.

Marketing Strategy: Focus on consumer-driven sub-categories like Art and Binders.

Sales Targets: Reassess targets for Furniture or optimize pricing/promotions.
